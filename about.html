The CUB story

I started CUB at 23 in 2015 because I was looking for a members club to join myself. Ten years on, I couldn't be prouder of what we have built: the largest private members club for business owners in Australia. Daniel Hakim · Founder & CEO

From day one, CUB was built with a clear purpose: to help members connect with the people most relevant to them and build a network that drives real growth. That belief remains at the core of everything we do.
